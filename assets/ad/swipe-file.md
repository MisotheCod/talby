## The 5 hook categories (Dan Koe framework — pick ONE per run, vary daily)

### 1. Harsh advice
Direct, no-sugar, productive discomfort. Patterns:
- "Stop [common behavior]. Start [better alternative]."
- "Your [excuse] isn't the problem. Your [real issue] is."
- "Nobody is coming to save you. [action]."
- Talby lens: "stop trusting your memory for who owes you money." / "nobody is going to chase your invoices for you." / "your 'system' is a spreadsheet you stopped updating in march."

### 2. Pain points (name the struggle so they feel seen)
- "You're not [negative label]. You're [reframe]."
- "Everyone talks about [getting the deal]. Nobody talks about [getting paid]."
- "The reason you're stuck: [specific insight]."
- Talby lens: "everyone talks about landing brand deals. nobody talks about the 14 tabs and 3 spreadsheets it takes to track them." / "the worst brand deal is the one you never got paid for and forgot about."

### 3. Counterintuitive truths (flip conventional wisdom)
- "Want to [goal]? Do the opposite: [action]."
- "The more you [common approach], the less you [outcome]."
- "[conventional wisdom] is wrong. Here's why."
- Talby lens: "a bigger brand deal list isn't success. knowing what you're actually owed is." / "the more confident you are that you've been paid, the less you actually know."

### 4. Key insights / big ideas
- "[concept] isn't about [common belief]. It's about [deeper truth]."
- "The difference between [A] and [B]: [insight]."
- Talby lens: "managing brand deals isn't about organization. it's about not leaving money on the table." / "the difference between a hobby creator and a business: one knows what they're owed."

### 5. Quotes / memorable lines
Standalone, quotable, shareable. Pull from our own voice and the product's promise.
- Talby lens: "one contract to check. one place for every deal." / "free for your first five deals" as a line.

## Psychological triggers to layer in (always, where natural)
- **Time-bound**: "in 30 days...", "this week...", "by next month..."
- **Transformation**: "become...", "know exactly...", "stop guessing..."
- **Exclusivity**: "most creators won't...", "few know..."
- **Status**: "a real business tracks this.", "join the ones who actually get paid."
- **Contrasting element**: small input → big output ("10 minutes a month → know exactly what you're owed"), or "one screen instead of 3 spreadsheets."

## Recreatable frameworks (structure templates that work)

### The Callout (proven 26k win, use most)
Open naming the exact painful behavior → one sharp verb → implied fix. No product mention until the reveal.
Frame: "[If you're doing X] this is your sign." / "[You're still doing X?]"
→ beat 1 hook, beat 2 the dashboard as the answer.

### The Either-Or (high engagement format)
Pose two types → which are you? → reveal Talby as the better option.
Frame: "there are two types of creators: the one who tracks every payment, and the one who is hoping. which one are you?"

### The Forgotten-Truth
Ask a question they can't answer → admit the answer surprises you → reveal the data.
Frame: "do you actually know what you've made this year? i didn't. turns out i was way off."
**PROVEN top (2026-10-05): `forgot_year` on this frame did 423 + 319 on YouTube Shorts — the dataset leader by ~6-8x. This + The Receipt is the #1 pairing right now. Favor on daily YT-Shorts pick.**

### The Receipt
Expose a quietly embarrassing normal → pull out the dashboard as proof.
Frame: "here's what nobody tells you about brand deals: most creators don't know how much they've actually made. i checked. it was bad." → reveal paid/outstanding.

## Rules (non-negotiable, carry into every hook)
- lowercase on-screen text, NO emojis, NO em dashes
- no hype words (seamless / effortless / revolutionize / unlock / game-changer / supercharge)
- the domain "www.talby.io" lives ONLY in the caption/title/on-screen card, NEVER spoken
- the actor says "Talby" at most once aloud
- never reuse a caption; tie it to today's hook
